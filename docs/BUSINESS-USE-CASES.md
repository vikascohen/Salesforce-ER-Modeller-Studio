# Business Use Cases

**Author: Vikas Cohen**

## What is Salesforce ER Modeller Studio for?

Salesforce organisations often grow over many years. New teams, projects and suppliers add objects, fields, relationships, automation and configuration. Eventually it can become difficult to answer a very simple question:

**What do we have, how is it connected, and what might be affected if we change it?**

Salesforce ER Modeller Studio is intended to make those questions easier to answer. It gives teams a visual and searchable view of their Salesforce structure so that people can understand an organisation before making changes.

The Studio deals with Salesforce architecture and metadata rather than business data. It is designed to support understanding, planning and decision making.

## Who can benefit from it?

The Studio can be useful to Salesforce architects, administrators, developers, delivery teams, technical leads, consultants, support teams and people responsible for Salesforce governance.

You do not need to understand how the Studio is built in order to use the information it provides.

## Business use cases

### Understand an existing Salesforce organisation

A Salesforce organisation may contain hundreds of objects and thousands of fields accumulated over many years. The Studio can help teams see the structure visually instead of trying to understand it one screen at a time.

This is useful when joining an existing programme, taking ownership of an organisation, beginning a transformation or reviewing a system that has grown over time.

### Understand what may be affected by a change

Before changing a field or part of the Salesforce structure, teams often need to understand where it is used.

The Studio brings available dependency information together so that a person can investigate what may rely on the item being changed. This can support better impact assessment and reduce the chance of discovering an unexpected dependency late in delivery.

The Studio provides evidence to support a review. It does not replace normal testing, change management or professional judgement.

### Support solution design

When planning new Salesforce capability, teams need to understand how a proposed design fits with what already exists.

The visual model can help people discuss where new objects and relationships belong, identify areas that are already complicated and compare a proposed design with the current organisation.

This can make architecture discussions easier because people are looking at the same picture rather than relying on separate assumptions.

### Improve change conversations

Business analysts, architects, administrators and developers may describe the same Salesforce area differently.

A visual model provides a common reference point. Teams can use it during design reviews, planning sessions and change discussions to explain how an area is structured and why a proposed change matters.

### Help with onboarding and knowledge transfer

Understanding a mature Salesforce organisation can take considerable time, particularly when documentation is incomplete or knowledge is concentrated with a few people.

The Studio can provide a starting point for someone learning the organisation by showing its structure, relationships and available architecture information in one place.

It can also help teams retain knowledge when people change roles or leave a programme.

### Create and maintain a Data Dictionary

Teams frequently need a simple way to understand what Salesforce objects and fields exist and what they represent.

The Data Dictionary brings this information together and can support documentation, architecture reviews, discovery work and discussions about existing configuration.

### Find areas that deserve architectural attention

Large Salesforce environments naturally develop areas with many relationships and dependencies.

Architecture Intelligence helps highlight structural patterns that may deserve a closer look. These are prompts for investigation, not automatic declarations that something is wrong.

This can help architecture teams decide where deeper review may provide the most value.

### Support governance and design assurance

Architecture and design governance often requires teams to understand the current environment before approving another change.

The Studio can provide supporting architecture information for design discussions and reviews. It can help reviewers ask better questions about impact, relationships and existing complexity.

It is a decision-support tool rather than an approval engine.

### Support modernisation and clean-up planning

Over time, Salesforce organisations can accumulate configuration whose purpose is no longer obvious.

The Studio can help teams investigate existing structure and dependencies before deciding whether something should be simplified, redesigned or considered for removal.

An absence of detected dependency evidence should not by itself be treated as proof that something is safe to delete. Normal analysis and testing are still required.

### Compare intended design with the organisation

Teams can model Salesforce structures visually and use the Studio to help reason about how intended architecture relates to the organisation they are working with.

This is useful during solution design, architecture workshops, platform modernisation and major programme planning.

## A simple example

Imagine a team wants to change an important Salesforce field.

Without a consolidated view, the team may need to search through several different parts of Salesforce and rely on people's knowledge to understand the possible impact.

With ER Modeller Studio, the team can start from the object and field, review the available usage and architecture information, see relevant relationships and use that evidence to guide the change assessment.

The value is not that the Studio makes the decision for the team. The value is that it helps the team make the decision with better visibility.

## What the Studio is not

ER Modeller Studio is not a replacement for Salesforce governance, testing, release management or architecture judgement.

It is also not intended to make automatic decisions about whether a design is good or bad. Architecture depends on business context, and a structural pattern that is appropriate in one organisation may be inappropriate in another.

The Studio is designed to make the Salesforce architecture easier to see, understand and discuss.

## The business value in one sentence

**Salesforce ER Modeller Studio helps teams understand what they have, see how it is connected, and make more informed decisions before they change it.**
